3. What would have been simpler with one agent using tools?

One agent could verify the rent context, prepare one request, stop for approval, and close after re-reading the paid record. Repeating the same evidence across Facilities, Accounting, and Boss added context overhead. For the tee, one agent could inspect exact-size stock and the linked invoice, account for shared rent, then prepare the missing records without recursive callbacks. For the hoodie request, one agent could compute the shortage/funding gap and store the final decision before drafting its explanation, avoiding the price/draft mismatch.

The dashboard's specialist contributions made responsibilities legible, and Inventory-first prevented a price-only answer to an infeasible order. Nonetheless, full connectivity caused avoidable requests and large final contexts. A single agent would simplify orchestration, not remove human approval, physical-evidence requirements, or the $104 deficit. Ticket 101's simulation was a human-authorized workaround, not an agent discovery.

5. Three problems requiring additional capabilities

  1. A damaged-item return and refund. Existing payment kinds cover invoices, rent, and purchases, not refunds. Customer Service, Inventory, and Accounting would need a sales/returns ledger, inspection and inventory-disposition tools, and a separately human-approved refund operation. A new agent alone would not provide those records.
  2. Verifying a hoodie supplier's actual quote and shipment. A Procurement role would need a verified SKU/supplier catalog, quote retrieval, and shipment-status tools with provenance. Real vendor contact is prohibited in this assignment; a permitted simulated source or changed scope would be needed. The authorized tee simulation is not a substitute for these integrations or physical receipt evidence.
  3. Repairing a leaking pipe in the shop. Facilities currently has lease/rent evidence, not work orders, contractors, estimates, or repair completion records. It would need maintenance and contractor tools plus human-confirmed completion; Accounting would need a supported repair obligation and guarded human payment approval.
